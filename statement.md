Problem Statement: The library staff encounters issues like an inefficient process, difficulty in tracking books, inaccurate data, and a substandard user experience due to slow service. This results in inefficiency among library staff and difficulties in managing books and integrating electronic media. 

Scope of Project: The scope of this project is to automate the system of library working staff in a way that it becomes less time-consuming and efficient. 

Target Users: Libray Staff

Features: The library staff will be able to perform the following operations:-

1.Add Book
2.Add Member
3.Borrow Book
4.Return Book
5.List All Books
6.List Available Books
7.List Borrowed Books
8.List All Members
9.Remove Book
10.Remove Member
